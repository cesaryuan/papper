# Equation attributes {#sec:equations}

The revised equation is @eq:revised; @eq:unchanged is an unchanged control.

$$
a + b = c
$$ {#eq:revised revision=true}

where $a$ and $b$ are the revised inputs.

$$
d + e = f
$$ {#eq:unchanged revision=false}

where $d$ and $e$ are unchanged inputs.

An unnumbered equation can also be marked as revised:

$$
g + h = i
$$ {revision=true}
