# Emphasis and revision styles {#sec:styles}

Plain text surrounds _emphasized words_ and **bold words**.
Nested emphasis combines _an emphasized phrase with **bold content**_.

The result includes [newly revised wording]{custom-style="Revision Char"}.

![Original figure caption.](figure.svg){#fig:original}

![[Revised figure caption with updated details.]{custom-style="Revision Char"}](figure.svg){#fig:revised}

Both @fig:original and @fig:revised remain available as numbered references.

A paragraph containing only inline math must keep its inline equation form:

$x_i$

Ordinary prose with inline math $y_i$ follows as a control.
